## **The Hacker's Brew.**

Hey there! I'm ***Sparsh***, your friendly neighborhood *Penetration Tester*, *Cybersecurity Enthusiast* and an *Analyst* at *CrowdStrike*.

Apart from using platforms like *TryHackMe*, *HackTheBox* or various *CTF Challenges* I like to keep my axe sharp by interacting with the community & writing fun blogs that relate to security and system administration. I am a part of the *(ISC)2 NoVA Chapter*, I co-own a *Discord server* that has more than 200 cybersecurity graduates from my university and have a pretty basic collection of [certificates](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hutgrabber/details/certifications/).

When I'm not trying to break into networks (ethically, of course) you can find me obsessing over the perfect cup of coffee, making travel plans, or mindlessly scrolling through [r/homelab](https://www.reddit.com/r/homelab/) on reddit.

## **The Tech Side.**

I would like to call myself an <u>offensive security practitioner</u> with expertise in <u>Linux</u>, <u>Windows</u>, and everything in between, life has it's own way of working out and I have nestled inn as a blue team <u>SOC Analyst</u> at CrowdStrike. While I daily-drive a <u>MacOS</u> notebook, I have a strong background in making and breaking both Windows and Unix systems alike - a skill that I have honed by learning as much <u>system administration</u> as I am able to.

I speak fluent *C/C++*, *Python*, *Bash*, and *PowerShell* amongst many other languages. Currently, I'm learning *Rust* & *Go* to see how their binaries work under the hood, hoping to learn how to reverse them.

Most of my experience is focused on <u>application security</u>, <u>system administration</u>, <u>network security</u>, <u>secure code reviews</u>, <u>forensics</u>, and <u>information assurance</u> with a fair amount of experience hardening both on-prem infrastructure and cloud deployments, such as <u>AWS</u> and <u>Azure</u>.
