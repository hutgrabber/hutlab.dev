### **The Hacker's Brew.**

Hey there! I'm ***Sparsh***, your friendly neighborhood *Penetration Tester*, *Cybersecurity Enthusiast* and an *Analyst* at *CrowdStrike*.

Apart from using platforms like *TryHackMe*, *HackTheBox* or various *CTF Challenges* I like to keep my skills sharp by interacting with others in the field of security. I am a part of the *(ISC)2 NoVA Chapter*, I co-own a *Discord server* that has more than 250 cybersecurity graduates from my university and in the future I look forward to attending various security conferences like *DefCON*.

When I'm not trying to break into networks (ethically, of course), you can find me obsessing over the perfect cup of coffee. That's right – I hack systems and brew beans with equal passion!

### **The Tech Side.**

 I am an offensive security practitioner with expertise in Linux, Windows, and everything in between. While I daily-drive a *MacOS* computer, I have a strong background in making and breaking *Windows* and *Unix* systems alike.

I speak fluent *C/C++*, *Python*, *Bash*, and *PowerShell* amongst many other languages. Currently, I'm learning *Rust* & *Go* to see how their binaries work under the hood, hoping to learn how to reverse them.

Most of my experience is focused on *application security*, *system administration*, *network security*, *secure code reviews*, *forensics*, and *information assurance*. I am skilled at hardening both on-prem infrastructure and cloud deployments, such as *AWS* and *Azure*.
